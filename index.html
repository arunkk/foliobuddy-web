Built for Real-World Portfolios

Most trackers stop at fund tickers or double-count partial disclosures. FolioBuddy models the full ownership graph.

01

Company Share-Class Unification

Own GOOGL directly while your ETFs hold both GOOGL (Class A) and GOOG (Class C)? FolioBuddy unifies multi-class shares via SEC CIK into a single Alphabet Inc. exposure record with every source path preserved.

02

Recursive Fund-of-Funds X-Ray

Target-date funds like VTINX hold other funds (VTI, VXUS, BND) inside them. FolioBuddy recursively traverses nested funds to surface leaf companies while separating Fixed Income and Cash sleeves.

03

Hybrid Mutual Fund Look-Through

When a mutual fund only discloses its top 10–25 stocks (e.g., 48% of assets) but publishes 100% Sector and Market Cap summaries, FolioBuddy allocates the remaining tail via Residual Fund Allocation—never double-counting known top stocks and always reconciling to 100%.

04

Two-Level Sector → Industry Drill-Down

Don't just see “Information Technology.” Drill directly into Semiconductors, Systems Software, Application Software, and Technology Hardware across direct and indirect holdings.

05

Market Cap Tier & 2D Heatmap Matrix

Slice your portfolio across Mega Cap (>$200B), Large Cap ($10B–$200B), Mid Cap, Small Cap, and Micro Cap—or inspect the 2D Sector × Market Cap matrix to spot hidden concentration.

06

Live Yahoo Finance + SEC EDGAR Engine

Automatically resolves live equity market caps, sectors, industries, SEC CIK parent entities, and ETF/mutual fund holdings with zero paid API keys required.
